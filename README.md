# Funding Facts

**Documenting foreign funding flows into Israeli civil society and politics — with a focus on European political foundations.**

This project collects publicly available information, reports, and primary sources about how government-linked European (especially German) political foundations and related organizations fund NGOs and activities in Israel.

The goal is transparency: to make the scale, mechanisms, and recipients of this funding visible to a wider audience, particularly in Europe.

---

## Core Findings (Summary)

- Germany has been one of the largest foreign governmental sources of funding to left-leaning and political advocacy NGOs in Israel over the past decade.
- Significant sums are channeled through **political foundations (Stiftungen)** affiliated with German parties represented in the Bundestag. These foundations receive the bulk of their budgets from the German government.
- Additional channels include church-related aid organizations and programs such as the Civil Peace Service (Forum ZFD).
- Israeli law prohibits political parties from receiving foreign donations or in-kind contributions. Funding to NGOs and "independent" centers creates a legal gray area that has been the subject of repeated criticism and investigations.

**Key reported figures (primarily 2012–2022 data from Im Tirtzu and media investigations):**
- At least **NIS 150 million** from German sources to left-wing / anti-Zionist organizations over roughly a decade.
- Of which approximately **NIS 30 million** came via the German political foundations.

### Media Investigations
- Israel Hayom (2022): "Following the money: How German state funds reach left-wing groups in Israel"
- JNS (2022): "German party funds pour dark money into Israel to influence domestic politics"

### Recent Case (November 2026)
- Channel 14 investigation: Allegations that suppliers for election rallies of "The Democrats" party were directed to receive payment via the **Willy Brandt Center** in Jerusalem, which is funded by **Forum ZFD** (a German organization receiving direct German government funding).
- Raises questions under Israel’s Party Financing Law regarding foreign in-kind contributions.

## The Mechanism

```
German Government budget
        ↓
Political Foundations (Stiftungen) / Forum ZFD / Church aid organizations
        ↓
Israeli NGOs, research centers, dialogue projects, and events
        ↓
Influence on public discourse, legal activity, and (in some alleged cases) political campaigning
```

The foundations are formally independent but receive the large majority of their funding from the German federal budget, allocated according to the parliamentary strength of the affiliated parties.
