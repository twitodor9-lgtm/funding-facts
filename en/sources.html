Notes on Methodology

Most precise numbers come from:

  1. Israeli NGO quarterly/annual reports to the Registrar of Associations
  2. Cross-referencing with foundation reports and media investigations
  3. NGO Monitor’s systematic tracking

Figures should always be checked against the original documents, as reporting practices vary and some foundations have been criticized for incomplete disclosure.
